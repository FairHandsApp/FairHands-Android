What FairHands is

FairHands is a platform where customers post jobs and independent providers (for example electricians, plumbers, cleaners, drivers and helpers) send offers. Providers are not FairHands employees. The provider you hire is responsible for the work.

Your account

You must be 18 or over, give true details and use your own mobile number. Keep your password private. You are responsible for activity on your account. One person, one account.

Providers

Providers must pass identity checks before sending offers, describe their skills honestly, quote fair prices, arrive when agreed and do the work safely and properly. Providers must have any licence or permission their work legally requires.

Prices and fees

The customer and provider agree the price in the app. Customers pay a small booking fee based on the job size and providers pay a service fee as a percentage of the job price. Current fees are shown in the app before you agree. Item costs for errands and shopping are passed on at cost.

Payments

Customers pay FairHands by Easypaisa, JazzCash or bank transfer, to the account shown in the app only. FairHands holds the money and pays the provider’s share after the customer approves the work, or automatically if the customer neither approves nor raises a problem within the time shown in the app. Do not pay providers outside the app; FairHands cannot protect or refund outside payments.

Cancellations, no-shows and disputes

Cancel through the app. If a provider does not arrive, or the work is not as agreed, report it in the app before approving. FairHands will review the job history, chat and photos and decide fairly on refunds or payouts. Refunds go back to the account the money came from.

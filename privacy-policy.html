Privacy Policy

This policy explains what FairHands collects, why, who can see it and the choices you have. FairHands connects customers in Pakistan with independent local service providers.

What we collect

Account: your name, mobile number and password (stored only in scrambled form). Profile: address or area, optional profile photo, language. Jobs: job details, photos you add, pick-up/drop-off points, offers, chat messages, reviews and ratings. Payments: the method, the number or account you paid from, an optional last 4 digits of the transaction and an optional screenshot. Providers also give: services, experience, public profile details, previous-work photos, payout account, and CNIC front, back and a selfie for identity checks. Support: messages you send us.

Location

When you allow it, the app uses your location to set your area and show nearby jobs or providers. Before a deal, others see only a rounded, approximate point and area, never your exact address. The exact job address is shown to the provider you hire, after the deal.

How we use it

To run your account and verify your phone; show jobs, offers and profiles; handle payments held by FairHands, refunds and payouts; check provider identity; prevent fraud and abuse; answer support requests; and send service messages (verification codes and important account or payment alerts).

Who can see what

Other users see what is needed for a job: your name, rough area, job details, offers, reviews and your optional profile photo. Phone numbers are not shown to other users, and contact details typed in chat are hidden. CNIC photos and payment evidence are private and only the FairHands team can see them. We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

Service providers we use

Supabase (secure database, sign-in and file storage), an SMS gateway for verification codes and alerts, Expo (delivering app updates) and OpenStreetMap (maps, which receive your device’s internet address when map tiles load). They process data only to provide these services.

Notifications on your phone

Notification read/archived status is stored on your device for each account. Clearing app data or reinstalling resets it.

How long we keep it

We keep account and job records while your account is active and as long as needed for payments, disputes, fraud prevention and legal duties. When you delete your account, your profile is removed from public view and personal data is deleted or anonymised, except records we must keep for money that was handled.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, remove your profile photo and your own job or previous-work photos, change your phone number, and delete your account from Account. You can also ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it through Help & contact.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so each person sees only what they are allowed to. No system is perfectly secure; never share your password or verification codes with anyone, including people claiming to be FairHands.

Children

FairHands is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly create accounts for children.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update the date below and, for important changes, tell you in the app. Questions: Account → Help & contact.

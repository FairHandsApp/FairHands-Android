Delete your FairHands account

In the app (fastest): open FairHands → Account → Edit profile → Delete account, enter your password and confirm.

Can't use the app? Ask from any phone: open FairHands, go to Account → Help & contact and send us a message, or contact the FairHands support team with your registered mobile number. We confirm the number is yours before deleting.

What is deleted

Your profile, name, phone number, address, profile photo, provider details, previous-work photos, ID (CNIC) photos and support messages are deleted or anonymised, and your profile disappears from the app.

What is kept, and for how long

Records of money that passed through FairHands (payment claims, refunds and payouts, with the job they belong to) are kept as required for accounting, disputes and the law, then deleted. Jobs that are still open or unpaid must be settled before the account can be deleted.
